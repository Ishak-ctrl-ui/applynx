• Growth 30D Sparklines: High-performance inline cubic bezier vector curves rendering live trends directly in every table cell with 0 external dependencies.
• Ad & Creative Intelligence: Scrapes and parses live YouTube creative footprints and direct link-outs to official Meta & Google ad libraries.
